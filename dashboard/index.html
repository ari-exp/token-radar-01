Farm

    Methodology

    Figures on this page are estimates from the 14 Nov 2026 snapshot, not a launch-price promise. Launch price cannot be known from TVL or FDV alone — initial liquidity, token distribution, demand, market makers, and the launch mechanism also determine the actual price.

    Formulas

    1. F1 Initial token price = FDV ÷ total supply (planned).
    2. F2 Launch market cap = price × circulating supply.
    3. F3 FDV = price × total supply (planned). If the token design allows minting beyond the plan, FDV must use maximum supply.
    4. F4 Circulating supply = total supply − locked tokens (vesting, team, treasury, staking).
    5. F5 Implied FDV = TVL × category multiple × size × growth × maturity × M_reputation.
    6. F6 AMM spot price = quote reserve ÷ token reserve — reference only, not computed from the snapshot (no reserve data).

    Example: FDV $100M, total supply 1B, circulating 150M (15%) → initial price $0.10, launch market cap $15M, FDV $100M.

    Launch price drivers: initial liquidity (partial, Market Liquidity slider proxy), token distribution (supply + circulating slider assumptions), demand (not measurable — scenarios only), market makers (no data), launch mechanism (unknown — AMM, bonding curve, LBP, IDO, or airdrop).

    Peer table by category

    Multiple = median mcap/TVL of tokenised protocols in the category (peers with mcap ≤ 0 discarded, ratios winsorized 0.01–10, min 2 peers, clamp 0.05–1.5; with fewer than 2 peers the computed global median 0.214 is used). Range = 25–75 quartiles of peer ratios (with fewer than 2 peers, the global middle half).

    What is not measured

    Revenue, funding, active users, volume, unlock/vesting schedules, AMM reserves, and market makers are not in the snapshot — the methodology names them instead of inventing their numbers. These formulas have never been tested against a real launch (no backtesting yet): all constants are judgement, not fitted results. The Risk Curators multiple uses the global median — its only peer (Api3, an oracle) is not economically comparable. Gauntlet: the kill-switch is on over the Resolv USR exploit of 22 Mar 2026; partial compensation still counts as not fully compensated.
